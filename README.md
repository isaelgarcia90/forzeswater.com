# ForzesWater

Create a modern, professional, fully responsive website for a water treatment company.
The goal of the site is to showcase the company’s expertise and their products: water pumps, control panels, filters, purification systems, and full water treatment plants.

General Style

Clean, modern, corporate design.

Use blue, cyan, turquoise, and white as the main color palette (water-cleanliness theme).

Rounded corners, minimal shadows, soft gradients.

Professional, trustworthy, eco-friendly visual identity.

High-quality icons and water-related illustrations (no childish look).

Use modern layout with large hero section, clear CTAs, and consistent spacing.

Overall Structure

Create a multi-section single-page website (or multi-page if Lovable prefers) with the following sections:

1. Header / Navigation

Company logo placeholder.

Navigation links: Home, About Us, Solutions, Products, Services, Projects, Contact.

Sticky navigation bar that remains visible on scroll.

2. Hero Section

Large full-width hero image related to clean water, purification, pipelines, or industrial water treatment.

A bold headline, such as:
“Professional Water Treatment Solutions for Industry, Commerce, and Residential Systems.”

Sub-text example:
“We design, manufacture, and install high-efficiency water treatment systems, pumps, filtration units, and purification plants.”

Main Call-To-Action (CTA): “Request a Quote”

Secondary CTA: “Explore Products”

3. About Us

Short company introduction with benefits bullets:

10+ years in water treatment solutions

Engineering-grade equipment

Turnkey installation and automation

Maintenance & technical support

Include a small image collage or icon-based info cards.

4. Solutions Section

Describe key water treatment solutions with modern icon cards or image blocks:

Water Pumping Systems

Filtration and Sedimentation Units

Reverse Osmosis Systems

Water Softening and Desalination Systems

Industrial and Commercial Water Treatment Plants

Automation and Control Panel Integration

Each item should have:

Title

Short description

Small icon or product photo

5. Products Section

Create a product gallery with categories:

Product Categories

Water Pumps (submersible, centrifugal, booster)

Control Panels (automation panels, PLC, pump controllers)

Industrial Filters (sand, activated carbon, cartridge)

Purification Plants (RO plants, wastewater treatment, drinking water plants)

Accessories & Spare Parts

Each product card includes:

Photo placeholder

Short technical description

Button: “More Details”

6. Services Section

Modern service cards describing:

Engineering & Design

Installation

Preventive Maintenance

On-Site Technical Support

Water Analysis & Consulting

Custom Manufacturing

7. Completed Projects (Portfolio)

Showcase project examples such as:

Industrial water treatment plant installation

Pumping station setup

RO purification system deployment

Control panel integration

Each project card:

Image placeholder

Location

Short description

Technologies or equipment used

8. Contact Section

Include:

Contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Message)

Map placeholder

Phone number

Email

Address

CTA: “Request a Quote Today”

9. Footer

Quick navigation links

Social media icons

Copyright

Company name

Contact info

Technical Requirements

Fully responsive (mobile-first).

Clean, semantic, accessible code.

SEO-friendly headings and structure.

Fast-loading images and optimized layout.

Include metadata for SEO related to:

water treatment

industrial water filtration

water pumps and purification systems

Tone

Professional, technical, trustworthy, environmentally conscious, focused on engineering and water quality.
